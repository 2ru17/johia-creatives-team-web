# johia-creatives-team-web

JOHIA Cubao Creatives Interactive Scrollytelling and Volunteer Onboarding Web Application.

## Architecture & Chapters

- **Chapter 01 - The Creative Studio & Palette Picker:** High-energy broken-grid studio hero (*"WE SHAPE THE VIBE"*), persistent 6-swatch palette switcher (Tangerine, Magenta, Cobalt, Canary, Emerald, Purple), and live telemetry header.
- **Chapter 02 - Multi-Angle DSLR Viewfinder & Shutter:** Procedural 3D sanctuary stage (`@react-three/fiber` + `@react-three/drei`), illuminated warm oak acoustic slats, boom microphone, acoustic worship guitar, synthesizer, focal presets (`35mm Prime`, `24mm Ultra-Wide`, `85mm Macro`), optical zoom, and interactive shutter flash.
- **Chapter 03 - Graphic Design & Lego Poster Assembler:** Scrollytelling deconstruction of a Sunday sermon key visual across 4 discrete layers (Base Carbon Slate, Silhouette Cutout, Modular Kinetic Typography, Studio Badges & Telemetry) with real-time 3D Explode view.
- **Chapter 04 - dau.lt Broken-Grid Portfolio Gallery:** Asymmetric 8-card showcase featuring real sermon and event campaigns (`Conqueror`, `Still Standing`, `When the Choice Is Yours`, `Interrupted`, `Larger Than Life`, `Beyond Your Capacity`, `Water Baptism`, `Wildfire Sunday`), authentic church documentary photography, and expandable BTS "Creative Recipe Breakdowns".
- **Chapter 05 - Documentation Write-Ups & Facebook Socials Hub:** Official Facebook page simulator (`@johiacubaoph`) with full-bleed cover banner, circular emblem, Sunday service time (10:00 AM), interactive reaction buttons, and dynamic editorial write-up tones.
- **Chapter 06 - Onboarding, Roles Bento & Google Form Registration:** 3-step training progression (Shadow, Co-Pilot, Lead Coverage), 4-track branch selector (Photography, Graphic Design, Write-Ups, Social Media), team distinction guide, direct Google Form integration, and scannable camera QR code.
